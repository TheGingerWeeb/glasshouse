Privacy

Short version: we keep nothing. Here is the longer version.

What happens to what you type

  • Your input is held in the server's memory for this scan only.
  • Results are wiped when you press Delete my results now, when the session expires ({{ (settings.job_ttl_seconds // 60) }} minutes), or when the server restarts.
  • There is no database, no analytics script, and no third-party tracking.
  • We never send your name or city anywhere. They are only used to build search links in your browser.

What leaves this server

  • Your username is sent to the public profile URLs being checked (that's how any username lookup works).
  • Your email is hashed and the hash is sent to Gravatar to see whether a public profile exists.
  • Nothing else leaves. Breach checks are links you click yourself.

What the host can see

This runs on a free hosting platform, so the platform sees network-level data such as your IP address and the pages you request. That is true of any website. Use a VPN if that matters to you.

What this tool will not do

  • Query breach databases or sell you results.
  • Scrape search engines, bypass logins, or probe password-reset endpoints.
  • Delete accounts for you — every deletion must be done by the account owner, logged in.
  • Check anyone but the person who consented on the form.

One honest warning

This is a public link. Anything it can reveal about you, someone else could reveal with the same free tools. That is not a reason to avoid it — it's the reason to fix what it finds.
